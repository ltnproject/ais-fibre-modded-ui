AIS Fibre Modded UI

Dark glassmorphism skin for the AIS Fibre router admin panel.
One-click bookmarklet — fully self-contained, no CORS issues.

Live GitHub ZTE F6107A · AIS Fibre by LtnProject

How it works

STEP 01
Drag the bookmarklet
Drag the green button below to your browser's bookmarks bar. One-time setup.
STEP 02
Visit your router
Open http://192.168.1.1 on your AIS Fibre Wi-Fi.
STEP 03
Click the bookmarklet
Click it from your bookmarks bar — the router page instantly transforms.
STEP 04
Login normally
Session token, captcha, and form — all 100% native. Nothing broken.

Install

Drag to your Bookmarks Bar

Drag the button below to your browser's bookmarks/favorites bar.
Then visit http://192.168.1.1 and click it.

Features

🌙
Dark Glassmorphism
Premium dark theme with frosted glass card, green glow & animated mesh.
🔑
Native Login
Session token, captcha & RSA all handled by the router — zero interference.
⚡
Self-Contained
No external script loading. All CSS inlined. Works even with strict CSP.
👁
Password Toggle
Show/hide password button injected into the password field.
📱
Responsive
Works on mobile and desktop browsers.
🔓
Open Source
